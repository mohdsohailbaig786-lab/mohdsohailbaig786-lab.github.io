01 · Engineering build

Liquid-cum-HEPA Air Purification System (Solar-Powered Hybrid Air Purifier)

Final-year diploma team project. Designed and built a two-stage hybrid air purifier for high-pollution Indian environments: a liquid wet-scrubbing stage (caustic soda NaOH solution) that neutralizes acidic exhaust gases and traps heavy soot before it can clog the filter, followed by a HEPA filtration stage that captures remaining fine particles (PM2.5, 0.3 micron standard). Powered by a 12V solar panel for zero running cost, designed for outdoor hotspots like traffic signals where power sockets are unavailable, with a digital display concept for AQI monitoring and public awareness.
